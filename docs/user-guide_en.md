# Worlds and Beyond — Quick Start (0.1)

Worlds and Beyond is your tool for pen-&-paper worlds: build first, then play.

At the top of the window you switch between two modes:

- **Edit — “RealmForge”:** where you build your world (game master / DM).
- **Play — “Adventure Nexus”:** the live session at the table (game master *and* players).

You reach the individual areas via the **icon bar on the left**.

---

## For Game Masters (DM) — “Edit” mode

- **🗂 Entries** — The heart of your world: characters, locations, factions, items, quests, scenes, cultures and lore. You create entries, fill in their fields and link them together. *Start:* 🗂 in the bar, pick a type on the left, then create a new entry.

- **🔍 Search** — Finds any entry across your whole world in seconds. *Start:* 🔍 in the bar or the shortcut **Ctrl/Cmd + K**, then type your query.

- **🗺 Maps** — Import map images and work with layers (background image, fog of war, tokens). Place markers/pins on locations and move figures (tokens). *Start:* 🗺 in the bar, “Import map”.

- **📅 Calendar** — Build your own world calendar (freely defined months and weekdays) and attach events to specific days. *Start:* 📅 in the bar, “Create new calendar”; clicking a day adds an event.

- **🎧 Audio** — A soundboard for music, ambience and effects in its own window — for atmosphere at the table. *Start:* 🎧 in the bar, “Start audio player”. *(Edit mode only.)*

- **🌌 Graph** — Shows your world’s web of relationships as an interactive knowledge graph: who knows whom, what belongs to what. *Start:* 🌌 in the bar. *(Edit mode only.)*

- **⚙ Settings** — Project management, appearance (light/dark and color themes), backup and app info. *Start:* ⚙ at the bottom of the bar.

---

## For Players — “Play” mode

- **Join (Lobby)** — How you get into your GM’s session: enter the invite link or code plus your display name, then connect. You then land in the play cockpit with the map the GM is currently showing. *Start:* when switching into “Play” mode as a player.

- **🗂 Entries** — View the entries your GM has shared with you (your character info, known locations and people). *Start:* 🗂 in the bar.

- **🔍 Search** — Search the content that is visible to you. *Start:* 🔍 in the bar or **Ctrl/Cmd + K**.

- **🗺 Maps** — See the map the GM is presenting and move your own figure (token). *Start:* 🗺 or the play cockpit.

- **📅 Calendar** — Keep an eye on the game world’s date and events, as far as they’ve been shared. *Start:* 📅 in the bar.

- **⚙ Settings (session context)** — Switch campaign or role, or leave the current session. *Start:* ⚙ in the bar.
